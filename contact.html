Before you write

A few quick answers

Where do I order the popcorn?

Two ways: through our Amazon store for Prime-friendly shipping, or through our website store where you can mix and match three kernel bags with any two seasonings. See the shop page for both.

Can I send a package as a gift?

Yes — every limited-edition package carries a personalized autograph from Eva on the product card, which makes them a natural gift for employees, loved ones, birthdays and holidays.

Do you offer wholesale?

We do talk wholesale with shops and stockists. Send us a message with your business details and the quantities you have in mind.

Can I pay by money order or cashier's check?

Yes. Download the mail-in order form from the shop page, fill it in, and post it with your payment to the address printed on the form.

How do I book Eva to speak?

Choose "Motivational speaking" in the form above and include your date, venue and audience, or visit the motivational speaking page for the full picture.
