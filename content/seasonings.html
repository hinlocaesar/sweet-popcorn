How to use them

Layer, don't drown

Salt first

As soon as the popping stops, hit the bowl with Buttery Popcorn Salt while the corn is still hot and slightly oily — it needs the heat to stick.

Then season

Add one savory or sweet seasoning on top. Start with a light shake, toss, taste, and repeat until it's where you want it.

Toss & serve

Give the bowl a good toss so every piece gets coated, then serve immediately while the crunch is at its peak.
