Directions for cooking our popcorn

It's real simple

Hands down, the best way to cook popcorn kernels is in a pan. Use an oil with a lower smoke point — many find coconut oil to be a great, healthy option.

Heat the oil

Pour one part oil into the pan, just enough to cover the bottom. Preheat it over a medium flame until it shimmers.

Add three parts corn

Drop in three parts popcorn — more or less to suit your taste — then lower the heat slightly and keep the pan moving.

Season & serve

Agitate until the popping stops. Tip into a bowl, shower with our Buttery Salt and finish with your favorite savory seasoning.
