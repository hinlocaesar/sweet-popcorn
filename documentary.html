The documentary of Eva's story

From world champion to popcorn maker

Three world boxing titles. Fourteen martial arts world championships. A nickname — "Sweet Magic" — that followed her out of the ring and onto a bag of popcorn.

The film follows Eva D. Jones-Young through the fights, the family and the small Indiana-sourced business she built with Larry, and what it takes to keep going long after the crowds go home. It is in production now, and the popcorn is helping pay for it.

  • In production with Eva's family and associates
  • Funded in part by every signed limited-edition package
  • Updates shared first with the documentary website and our YouTube channel
