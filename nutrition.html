Why we publish them

Read it before you eat it

Popcorn is one of the simplest foods in the world — so the label should be simple too. Our kernels are non-GMO, nut free and contain no dyes or preservatives, and every seasoning carries a full Nutrition Facts panel you can actually read.

  • All six kernel varieties share the same nutritional value
  • Buttery Popcorn Salt: 32 servings, 1 Tbsp (14g) per serving
  • No artificial colors, flavors or preservatives anywhere in the range
