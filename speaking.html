Eva is now available for motivational speaking

A 3X world boxing champion and 14X martial arts world champion who turned a nickname into a business — Eva brings the same discipline to the podium that she brought to the ring.

Who you're booking

Eva "Sweet Magic" Jones-Young

Champion, coach, founder and storyteller. Eva has spent decades competing at the highest level — and the last few years turning everything she learned into a business her whole family helps run.

She speaks from experience rather than theory: what preparation looks like when nobody is watching, how you come back from a loss, and why the smallest, most ordinary things — a bag of popcorn, a signed card, a returned email — are where character actually shows up.
