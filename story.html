Our story

Two hands, one state of mind

Eva and her husband Larry are "hands on" when it comes to their popcorn. Only the finest natural kernels come from their hometown and state of Indiana.

The documentary Shop the popcorn
Eva & Larry — hands on, start to finish.
A family operation

Hands on, every single bag

Established in 2024, Sweet Magic Popcorn was born out of a passion for sharing unique and delicious popcorn kernels — along with a savory buttery salt and mouth-watering seasonings to go with it all.

Eva and Larry don't hand the work off. They choose the kernels, they pack the boxes, and they sign the gift packages themselves. It's a small operation on purpose: it keeps the product honest and it keeps the story theirs.

Hometown & home state

From the good ol' farmland of Indiana

Only the finest natural kernels come from Eva and Larry's hometown and state of Indiana — six varieties, all non-GMO, nut free, and free of dyes and preservatives.

  • Red, white, blue, purple, mushroom & rainbow — one-pound bags
  • Sourced from American farmers in the heart of the Hoosier state
  • Packed by hand and shipped nationwide
