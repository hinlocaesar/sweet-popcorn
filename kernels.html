Profiles of a colored kernel

Colored kernel popcorn & mushroom popcorn

Once popped, will they still be the color of their kernel?

No. While our uniquely-colored popcorn kernel varieties have a colored kernel, they will be white or yellow once popped. For popped popcorn to be colored, the kernels need to be dyed beforehand — and we don't use dyes or preservatives.

Do all of the colors taste the same?

No — they each have distinct flavors. Our Blue popcorn often has a slightly sweet taste, more pronounced than traditional yellow or white popcorn. Purple is known for its mild flavor, and Red has a slightly sweeter taste compared to the others.

What does "hulless" actually mean?

Hulless popcorn is known for its softer and more delicate texture by definition. Sweet Magic's white popcorn varieties are all hulless, so they have a thin kernel shell — a melt-in-your-mouth quality that is much easier on people with sensitive teeth.

The difference between white & yellow kernels

Three things that set them apart

Color

The most obvious difference — and unlike colored-kernel popcorn, the popped piece is the same color as the kernel. Yellow pops yellow, white pops white.

Flavor

Yellow popcorn has a much more robust, buttery flavor. White popcorn is neutral and mild — a blank canvas for whatever flavor you desire. Yellow is the go-to for movie-theater style.

Texture

Yellow popcorn has a denser hull, so it's chewier with a satisfying crunch. Sweet Magic's hulless white varieties have a tender, melt-in-your-mouth quality instead.
